## Where the project stands

The direction changed materially today. Plutus is no longer being framed as a
"fill model" or a "backtest auditor". It is:

> **A simulated Vietnamese exchange a strategy connects to like a broker API.**
> Submit orders, receive status, and the exchange remembers settlement, margin and
> the rulebook so the strategy author does not have to.

Not a backtesting engine — no strategy execution, no portfolio, no P&L. The caller
owns those. Plutus is the counterparty.

Horizon: **six months (Sep 2026 – Feb 2027)**, not the 31 Aug RIVF deadline. The
deadline is real but explicitly non-binding; delivering the full purpose matters more.
The live "Calibrator" gold-standard validation is deferred beyond this window.

## Decisions already made — do not relitigate

1. Multi-exchange sessions, specifically to support **pair trading** (VN30 basket vs VN30F).
2. Derivatives margin sits in a **segregated deposit account** with independent
   purchasing power. No auto-transfer exists in Vietnam; the caller must transfer.
3. *Ứng trước tiền bán* (advance on sale proceeds) is a **broker term**, configurable,
   with a daily interest rate.
4. Fees and taxes **are modelled**, configurable, and per-venue — a generic charge
   table, not constants.
5. Fill determination is a **pluggable policy** (hard / soft / probabilistic). This is
   the selling point: run one strategy under all three and report the spread.
6. Synchronous `submit`/`poll` for now; event-driven is future work.
7. Both daily-bar and tick resolution, driven by what the data source declares.
8. No market impact. Ever. State it in every result.

## Live bugs found today, verified against source or corpus

- `margin.py:40,42` — `vsd_initial = 0.175` matches **no** published VSD value at any
  date (the real series is 10% → 13% → 17%), and **Vietnam publishes no maintenance
  margin ratio at all**. The requirement is `MR = IM + VM` tested as utilisation
  against 80/90/100% thresholds, with variation margin counted only when in loss. The
  whole shape is wrong, including the derived 6.06% trigger.
- `constant.py:92` — `is_current` is inclusive at both ends, so 09:15:00, 11:30:00,
  13:00:00 and 14:30:00 belong to two sessions at once.
- `constant.py:36` — `TRADING_UNIT[HSX] = 100` for all history. The HSX round lot was
  **10** until 2021-01-03. The corpus holds 94,675 HSX stock closes in 2020 (verified),
  so `ROUND_LOT` currently rejects orders the real HOSE accepted.
- `constant.py` `get_hsx_tick_size` — the 8-char C/E/F override wrongly catches closed-end
  funds. Verified: all 151 `FUC*` closes in the corpus are multiples of 50, so they use
  the banded table, not a flat tick.
- `exchanges/equity.py` `_admits_in_session` — refuses a **legal LO** in every call
  auction on every venue. HOSE's own session table reads "LO, ATO".
- `data/audit.py` — the tick-grid check skips non-HSX venues, claiming it would be
  vacuous. Refuted: **15,504 UPCOM closes are off the 100đ grid** across 30 tickers.
- `adapters/datahub.py:210` — hardcodes `foreign_room=None`, and the comment at
  `exchanges/equity.py:101` claiming the corpus has no such field is **false**.

## Corrections to earlier claims (do not repeat them)

- **Foreign room does bind.** An earlier pass concluded it never does, from "min room 1,
  zero rows at 0". Wrong test — the rule is *room < order quantity*, and **34,653 rows
  sit below a single 100-share lot**. FPT on 2022-12-30 runs down to 11 shares.
- **Equity settlement has been T+2 since 2016-01-01** (VSD Decision 211/QĐ-VSD), not
  since 2022. The 2022 decisions changed the time of day, not the cycle length.
- **A settlement calendar IS required.** T+2 counts VSDC settlement business days, which
  diverge from trading days around Tết. The spec's "holiday-correct by construction"
  claim is wrong.
- **"T+1.5" is not a regime name.** Call it "T+2 with mid-day settlement".
- `Exchange.sustains()` takes a whole price path in one batch call and cannot be the
  session's per-day mark. It stays untouched; the session aggregates `margin.py`.

See the memory files (`~/.claude/projects/-Users-nadan-algotrade-research-plutus/memory/`),
especially `order-book-data-exists.md` and `validation-oracle-findings.md`. Highlights:

  (`quote_bidprice.csv` / `quote_askprice.csv`, ~97M/95M rows, 2021-01-15 → 2022-12-30).
  Sizes are backed up separately; the re-backfill is trivial per the user.

  per ticker-day; only the top liquidity quintile supports intraday work.
- Band-dependent results are inherently **2021–2022** (`quote_ceil` starts 2021-02-05).
