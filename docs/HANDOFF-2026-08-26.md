# Handoff — 2026-08-26

Read this first if you are picking up without the prior conversation. It supersedes
`HANDOFF-2026-08-25.md`, which is now stale in several load-bearing ways.

## What Plutus is

> **A simulated Vietnamese exchange a strategy connects to like a broker API.**
> Submit orders, receive status, and the exchange remembers settlement, margin and the
> dated rulebook so the strategy author does not have to.

Not a backtesting engine — no strategy execution, no portfolio, no P&L. The caller owns
those. Plutus is the counterparty.

## State: Tier 1 and Tier 2 are built and green

**1255 tests passing.** `src/plutus/market/session/` is the simulator:

| Module | What it owns |
|---|---|
| `types.py` | The shared contract. Transition graph, time-in-force table and terminal triggers as **data**, so no module can draw a second graph |
| `rulebook.py` | Per-instant resolution. A venue is `(ticker, ts)`. KRX is a dated edition. Resolution is total: `KNOWN` / `NOT_APPLICABLE` / `UNKNOWN`, never a guess |
| `calendar.py` | VSDC settlement business days. Reproduces the rulebook's own Tết 2026 worked example |
| `ledgers.py` | Encumbrance + tranche holdings + tranche proceeds + the sale advance |
| `orders.py` | The state machine, per-order-type terminal edges |
| `deposit.py` | Segregated deposit; `account_margin_requirement` takes the **whole account** |
| `fills.py` | `FillPolicy` seam: hard, soft, probabilistic, plus an explicit `INDETERMINATE` |
| `charges.py` | Generic dated charge table, per venue |
| `corporate.py` | Dividends, splits, bonus, rights |
| `exchange.py` | `ExchangeSession`: submit / cancel / amend / poll / advance_to |

The headline behaviour works end to end and is pinned: **buy at T0, try to sell the same
day, the exchange refuses because the shares have not settled** — with the mirror test
proving the same sell is accepted once the tranche settles.

## The five locked shapes — and how to verify them

These are the design decisions that cost a rewrite if wrong. Every one was verified the
only way that actually tests it: **one session straddling a regime boundary**, not two
sessions run at two dates.

| Straddle | Result |
|---|---|
| HSX fee 0.0003 → 0.00027 @ 2020-03-19 | 28,650 then 25,785 |
| Round lot 10 → 100 @ 2021-01-04 | `Accepted` then `Rejected(ROUND_LOT)` |
| Settlement → T+2 @ 13:00 @ 2022-08-29 | two parcels in **one** `Holding` |
| VSD IM 0.13 → 0.17 @ 2022-12-15 | PIT 6,500 then 8,500 |

1. **Per-instant resolution.** Forbidden: config-at-load singletons, ticker-keyed venue
   caches. Twelve survivors were catalogued and four fixed; the eight remaining are
   documented as latent with the reason each is harmless.
2. **Encumbrance ledger** — reserve on accept, release on *every* terminal edge.
3. **Tranche-list holdings and proceeds.** Forbidden: scalar pairs.
4. **The order type IS the time-in-force**, per-type terminal edges.
5. **Margin takes the whole account.** `account_margin_requirement(Position(...))` raises
   `TypeError` by design.

## The paper's claim — read this before writing a word of it

Eleven agents surveyed prior art, three tried to refute each claim, and a twelfth
verified the survivors against PDFs and APIs. **Most of what we intended to claim did not
survive.** Full detail in `docs/reference/literature-review.md`; the outline is
`docs/reference/paper-outline.md`.

**Dropped entirely, refuted:**
- *Pluggable fill determination.* NautilusTrader ships `prob_fill_on_limit` in {0, p, 1}
  by default. LEAN, Zipline, RQAlpha, hftbacktest, PyAlgoTrade too.
- *Data-source agnosticism via a published contract.* LEAN's `BaseData`, with
  `SupportedResolutions()` and `IsSparseData()`, is exactly that.
- *Being first to an explicit indeterminate outcome.* Forex Strategy Builder shipped
  `enum BacktestEval {Error, None, Ambiguous, Unknown, Correct}` with a Method Comparator
  **in 2011**.

**Must be hedged, never asserted flat:**
- ~~"first to combine call auctions, price limits and settlement"~~ — **EvoMarket**
  (arXiv:2604.18046, 20 Apr 2026) does exactly that for China A-shares.
- ~~"first matching-engine simulation of a Vietnamese exchange"~~ — Lê Đức Hùng (2013,
  VNU-UET) built a QuickFIX HNX test double.
- The intrabar-ambiguity framing belongs to **Claeys** (SSRN 6240638, verified exact:
  18.47% ambiguous bars, 3,695-point spread).

**The lead claim, which survived attack:**

> Plutus represents a national exchange rulebook as **effective-dated rule editions
> resolved per simulated instant**, so a 2024 backtest and a 2026 backtest execute under
> the rules that actually applied to each — across a real regime change, the KRX cutover
> of 2025-05-05 — and we find no simulator, open-source or commercial, in which any
> **admission** rule is keyed to an effective date.

Its warrant is measured, not asserted: `measurements/dated_rules.py` finds **82.2% of the
HSX equity sample (893,628 of 1,086,518) predates the round-lot change**, so a date-blind
simulator rejects every legal 10-to-90 share order across four fifths of it. Vo & Doan
(2023, PLoS ONE) supply the independent warrant via a difference-in-differences on HOSE's
real 2016 tick-size change.

**Social risk:** the JICCE dashboard paper (Le Hoanh-Su et al., UEL/VNU-HCM) published at
RIVF 2025 and we submit to RIVF'26. Assume they are in the reviewer pool. Cite accurately
and respectfully.

## Results worth not re-deriving

- **Settlement.** `quote.settlementprice` holds the **already-computed** value; the last
  entry on an expiry day *is* the settlement price — read it, do not average it. Measured
  across all 46 post-cutover expiries, the close-as-settlement substitution costs mean
  absolute **0.042%**, worst **0.333%**. VN30F2206, the contract earlier notes leaned on,
  sits near the *worst* of the distribution.
- **Venue mis-attribution.** Extending the tick-grid audit past HSX finds 15,504 off-grid
  UPCoM closes — and **all 15,504 lie exactly on HOSE's grid**. They are HOSE prices with
  a UPCoM label. Two unrelated methods (tick grids here, price bands in the rulebook)
  converge on the same defect and name the same tickers.
- **The two margin paths disagree**, and this is reported rather than resolved away. The
  account model calls 100% of the 381 entries against a published 7.61 / 12.60 / 14.70%,
  and no funding multiple reproduces all three counts jointly. Retirement of the legacy
  path is **pending a decision**, not assumed.

## Standing rules for this project

1. **Overclaiming is a defect.** Every unsourced value says so in its own docstring; see
   `BrokerTerms.PROVENANCE` and `margin.PROVENANCE` for the pattern.
2. **Exchange rules and broker terms live in separate objects.** Gazetted-and-dated
   versus commercial-and-per-firm. Never assert a house rule as market law.
3. **Where the rulebook is silent, say so** rather than inventing a rule.
4. **A fix is not done until a test fails without it.** The suite was green through every
   one of the twelve defects found by adversarial review — green tests prove very little
   on their own.
5. **Discarded experiments get recorded.** See the tick-grid dead end in
   `measurements/dated_rules.py`: an invented rule table produced a large, convincing,
   meaningless number.
